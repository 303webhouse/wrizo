## ITEM 2 · TRR11'S RELEVANCE GATE

**The question:** does the STYLE GUIDE dropdown ride the citation ask's gate — appearing
only when the citation ask is in play — or stand in Revise always?

**The desk's recommendation: split it, and neither answer alone.** The style *authority*
(MLA default; Chicago, APA, AP) is a **per-project setting** that lives in T7 and is set
once, the way a writer picks a style for a manuscript rather than per question. The
*dropdown in the panel* then **rides the citation ask's gate** and appears only when
citation is the subject at hand. Reasoning: a permanent dropdown in Revise is chrome
for an occasional need, and this arc's whole discipline is absence over disabled; but a
setting the writer can never reach until they need it is worse — they cannot establish
the authority before asking under it. Split, both problems disappear.

## ITEM 3 · CITATION DEPTH

**The question:** does the citation ask check **form only** — is this citation shaped
correctly for the chosen authority — or also **ledger completeness**, whether the work
cites everything it draws on?

**The desk's recommendation: form only, for now, and say so in the copy.** Form is
checkable from the citation itself. Completeness is not: it requires knowing what
sources the writer consulted, which the app has no honest way to know — it would either
guess from the prose (unreliable, and an accusation when wrong) or demand a source list
the writer never agreed to keep. An honest instrument that does less beats a confident
one that infers. Completeness stays a named future capability if a source ledger ever
becomes a thing the writer maintains on purpose.
