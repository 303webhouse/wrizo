## FIRST PASS

### THE EXPERTS — the line, drawn

**Locate, diagnose, direct — never supply.** A lawful editing suggestion has three
verbs. It **locates** — points at the writer's real text, quoting it back if useful
(reference atoms, already lawful). It **diagnoses** — names what the text is doing, in
craft terms. It **directs** — names the *kind* of change worth considering. What it
never does is supply the replacement: not a sentence, not a clause, not a word. "The
second paragraph repeats the first's rhythm — vary one" is guidance. "Change 'walked
slowly' to 'ambled'" is a one-word ghostwrite: the replacement atom is the page's first
foreign byte, and A13 exists so there is no road for it to travel. This converts the
SYSTEM_PROMPT's reply-side law into menu-side law: **no chip may be phrased to elicit
composition.** The wall polices answers; the menu now polices questions.

**Draft admits located craft asks; verdicts still wait.** The gradient across the
three passes: Free Write allowed momentum shapes only (TFW4); Draft admits asks about
*specific stretches and aspects* — where it drags, what carries, where the thread
slips; Revise, last, is where evaluation proper must make its argument, if it can
clear the same wall. "Is this good" remains out of Draft — not because judgment is
forbidden forever, but because a verdict on the whole belongs to the mode whose verb
is judging, argued behind the sitting.

### THE ARCHITECTS — the mapping

### THE OPPOSITION — named chair: Marketing / simplicity

**Objection 1 — the line is too fine to survive contact.** Writers will edit a staged
chip into "just rewrite it" anyway, and the tutor will refuse; a menu that stages
questions the system then declines is a trap dressed as help. **Answer:** the refusal
side already exists and already teaches — the SYSTEM_PROMPT declines composition by
offering the lawful counsel instead. The chips model the ask-shape that never needs
declining; a writer absorbing that shape is the product working. **Conceded, and
carried:** the decline's copy quality is load-bearing — it must teach, never scold —
flagged as O-TD1 for the build brief.

**Objection 2 — Draft's Counsel is creeping toward a sidebar IDE.** Four chips and a
wakened lens; FW's restraint is already eroding. **Answer:** the whole delta from
Free Write is one chip and one default — and Draft is the mode where the writer asked
for working tools by choosing it. The ceiling holds. **Partially conceded:** if the
sitting reads Draft's panel as loud, Consistency returns to rest — a one-default
change, sitting on the lock sheet as exactly that.

**Objection 3 — the selection ask is a Trojan horse.** Today a read; tomorrow "apply
the suggestion" is one button away. **Answer:** the horse has no door. Apply would
require an editor reference or a text setter, and the component receives neither —
the wall is architectural, harness-asserted, and this lane's charter bars the wiring
its whole design forbids. The read itself waits for ratified consent language before
a byte moves. What the objection truly names is vigilance for future passes, and the
record now carries it.

### THE WORKING-WRITERS BENCH

Draft's real want, in the testimony's own shape: a second pair of eyes that names
*function*, not fixes. "Tell me what's working" matters as much as what isn't — ask
two is deliberately two-sided ("load-bearing… and what could go") because shaping is
selection, not just repair. And the bench's warning mirrors Free Write's: diagnosis
delivered as verdict kills drafts the way the critic kills sessions. Every Draft ask
is phrased at stretches and functions — never at the writer, never at the whole.

### THE COGNITION / ADHD BENCH

Four chips is still a glance — one row, short labels, fixed order, no reshuffling.
The selection chip holds its slot at all times, disabled-visible without a selection
(G3's lawful species) rather than appearing and vanishing — layout stability outranks
purity of absence for a row whose gate flips by the second. Staging stays instant;
no motion enters the composer.
