### E1 — the rail menu does not shut the tool sliver on boards

Ledger, verbatim: *"the **TWO-HANDS RULE not working** — a far-left open does
NOT shut the tool sliver — a ruled wave requirement, now a wave defect."*

**Cause, located:** `apps/desktop/src/store/menusDrawers.ts:74`

```js
const band = paper.getBoundingClientRect().left - rail.getBoundingClientRect().right;
if (!Number.isFinite(band) || band <= 0) return true;
```

`canCoexist()` measures the band between the rail's right edge and the
paper's left edge, and returns `true` — *permissive* — when that band is zero
or negative. On a **full-bleed canvas** the board has no inset paper, so the
band is `<= 0` and the guard reads it as "they may coexist" when what it
actually means is "there is no room at all." The two-drawer law is inverted
exactly where it matters most.

The fix is not to flip the return. The degenerate case needs its own branch:
a zero band on a full-bleed surface and a zero band from a failed measurement
are different facts, and `Number.isFinite` is already carrying one of them.

### E2 — the Tutor panel loses its right-edge anchor under board resize

Ledger, verbatim: *"the **TUTOR PANEL does not stay anchored to the board's
RIGHT EDGE under board resize** — Nick's ruling: it should."*

The Tutor panel's right-edge anchor does not survive a board resize. Under
the anchor law this is a layout fault, not a policy one: **an anchor may not
measure.** The repair belongs in CSS, not in a resize handler that writes a
position — writing one would be the exact failure the law exists to prevent.
