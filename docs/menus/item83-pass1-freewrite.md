## §0 · THE CONTEXT

Free Write is the funnel — mode key `journal`, the posture not the place (FX1 S3). The
writer is producing words forward; the surface cannot wrap a selection; the back-out
moments this mode owes are the fewest of any context. Chamber 1's ratified roster, his
ruled naming quoted whole: *"the typewriter cluster ONLY — the Typewriter icon opens
Forward Momentum / Text Fade / Page Scroll toggles; 'Controls' and 'Forward lock' as
labels retire. Plus the goal block. Nothing else — this stage produces words."* This
pass does not re-argue that law. It maps it into the grammar and names what the mapping
costs.

## FIRST PASS

### THE EXPERTS — why the barest desk

**"Nothing else" is the design.** Every context pass after this one argues what to
include; this one argues what including *nothing* looks like when done well. Free Write
is the purest test of G7 (closed cost = one grip) and of the funnel thesis itself: the
mode exists to make forward motion cheaper than fiddling. A desk that offers emphasis
buttons during the funnel is offering the fiddle.

**The ratified roster is already grammar-shaped.** The cluster is an instrument's
toggles; the goal block is an instrument; both are foot-species in Pass 0's anatomy.
Chamber 1, read through G1's third-kind lens, ruled that Free Write's Desk holds
**instruments only** — no sections of tools above the foot at all. The mapping writes
itself, which is the strongest evidence the grammar and the ruling agree.

### THE ARCHITECTS — the mapping

**The panel is the foot.** Free Write's Desk panel, open, shows the shared foot alone:
the goal block (timer from first keystroke, hairline, inline target) and the instruments
row. No sections mount above it on an ordinary page. The grip, the panel geometry, the
vanish behavior — all inherited unchanged from the substrate (§1 of the census; the
three-regime law binds).

**The Typewriter assembles as ruled.** The foot's typewriter control becomes the
**Typewriter instrument button**; its press opens the ruled three-toggle cluster —
**Forward Momentum · Text Fade · Page Scroll** — as a pop-out, exactly one level deep
(G4's ceiling, met precisely by the ruling's own shape: "the Typewriter icon opens").
"Forward lock" retires as a label; `railForwardLock`'s toggle becomes Forward Momentum's.
Three rows, three visible states, no prose inside the pop-out. **Fade depth stays in the
gear** — the cluster holds the on/off of Text Fade; the gear tunes how deep. The
toggle/tuning split is defensible but the two fade homes must read as toggle-vs-tuning
on hardware or be merged by Nick's word — flagged for the sitting below.

**Format retires from Free Write.** The ratified "nothing else" excludes the built
Bold/Italic pair, and the two-press bracket retires with it — the bracket existed only
because a forward-only surface can't wrap a selection, and it has no other reason to
live. Emphasis mid-funnel is typed as the convention itself (`*word*` — the storage law
makes typed markdown first-class, and the dimmed-syntax register already renders it),
or applied in Draft, where the full set lives. **This is FW3 below, the pass's one
retirement of a built control; its consequence is named there in full.**

**The Ink-tool placeholder exits.** FX7 S2 placed it as a *reasoned* inert placeholder —
present-but-disabled, disclosed by tooltip. Chamber 1's later roster excludes it, and
G3 names the grammar reason: a grayed control for an unbuilt capability is a locked door
wearing paint. The supersession is by name, nothing silent: **FX7 S2's placement yields
to Chamber 1's roster and G3**; when ink is real, its home gets argued as a real
capability's home (the HD charter keeps Ink/Image, census §9.2).

**Journal origin-furniture survives — as an origin-gated section.** The Ink swatches,
nib stub, and capture items ("Spark deck" · "Fragments" · "Send → Drawer") are gated on
the page's *origin*, not the mode — outside Chamber 1's subject, which was the per-mode
strips. The grammar already holds the right species: the Board's sliver section is
absent-not-disabled on system boards; the same law applied to origin gives journal-born
pages one **"Journal"** section above the foot, and every other page none — absent, not
disabled, not explained. On the capture items' kind: "Send → Drawer" routes content
through a door while the writer stays — a routing instrument, not a door. It keeps its
G1 home in the hand.

**The Counsel side, framed only.** The Tutor's grip and panel ride the grammar
unchanged; its contents are the Tutor lane's (84). One Free-Write frame rule belongs
here: **nothing arrives unbidden during the funnel.** Nudges stay asleep (FX12; the

**Out of scope, named:** the mode strip itself (doors and modes, BM1's territory — the

### THE OPPOSITION — named chair: Marketing / simplicity

**Objection 1 — retiring Format guts the italic thought.** Writers feel emphasis
mid-flow; taking the buttons reads as the app disapproving of feeling. And typed
markdown is programmer dialect, not writer dialect. **Answer:** the storage law makes
`*word*` the page's own honest tongue, and the dimmed-syntax register renders it as
craft, not code. The deeper answer is Chamber 1's own: this stage produces words — the
buttons were the fiddle. **Conceded honestly:** if the sitting shows Nick's own hand
reaching for italics in Free Write, FW3's consequence line is where his overrule lands
cleanly; the roster is his to amend, and the pass keeps that door visibly on its hinges.

**Objection 2 — the cluster pop-out taxes the most-used toggles.** Momentum toggles get
flipped often; hiding three toggles behind one press adds friction exactly where flow
lives. **Answer:** the press is one, from a persistent foot on a persistent grip — and
the ruling's shape is Nick's own ("the Typewriter icon opens"). The chord (`Ctrl/Cmd+/`)
plus one press reaches any toggle without the pointer leaving the desk. The sitting
verifies the feel; the mapping doesn't pre-empt it.

**Objection 3 — an all-foot panel looks broken.** A writer opens the hand and sees…
instruments and no tools; the panel reads as unfinished. **Answer:** it reads as a desk
cleared for work — and the goal block plus instruments row is not visually empty. The
one honest risk is a writer hunting for Bold; the Draft tab is one press away and is
where editing lives. Flagged for the sitting rather than padded with furniture.

### THE COGNITION / ADHD BENCH

Free Write is the momentum-critical mode; the bench's rulings are the strictest here.
**Zero unbidden events:** no arrival animation beyond the one engine, no nudges, no
counsel motion during typing. **Instant-read cluster:** three rows, three visible
states, no nested text — a glance, not a read. **Origin section collapsed by default**
on journal pages, expansion remembered (the memory law; re-scanning is the tax).
**The goal flare stays evental** — earned, wordless, outliving nothing (M4's law).
