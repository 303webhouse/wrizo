## §2 · WHAT EACH RULING SETTLES — supersessions named

**R1 · FREE WRITE GAINS "STYLING": Bold · Italic · Underline.** Overrules
Chamber 1's *"nothing else"* for Free Write and Pass 1's FW3 (the Format
retirement) by founder word — the two-press bracket semantics RETURN with it
(a forward-only surface still cannot wrap a selection). **Underline is a new
citizen of the set:** it has no frozen-set convention, so the storage law needs
one word (flag F2 below). The frozen markdown set is hereby the *founder's
set*: his word extends it, exactly as the freeze always provided.

**R2 · FREE WRITE GAINS "INK", below STYLING.** Pen/pencil types and ink
colors, standing in the panel for the stylus features' return. Supersedes
FW4 (the placeholder's exit) *in the opposite direction* — ink is now a ruled
section, not a placeholder — and supersedes FW5's origin-gating for ink
(the section is Free Write's on every page, not journal-furniture). The
capture items (Spark deck · Fragments · Send → Drawer) were not ruled on and
remain origin-gated as passed. Mounting rides the stylus arc's return
("for when we get back"); the design is settled now.

**R3 · THE TYPEWRITER GROWS SETTINGS.** The Typewriter button opens its
pop-out; inside it, each behavior carries its toggle AND its adjustments:
**Forward Lock** — on/off, window customizable by unit (words · sentences ·
etc.) and count; **Line Fade** — on/off, lines shown, and the writing line's
position (top · center · bottom of the page). Adjustments open as in-place
disclosure INSIDE the one pop-out, so G4's one-deep ceiling holds. This
resolves O-FW2 by merger: the gear's old "Fade depth" folds into Line Fade's
settings; no second fade home survives. Page Scroll was not ruled on and
rides in the pop-out as before. **Naming supersession, named loudly:** Chamber
1 retired "Forward lock" for "Forward Momentum" and named "Text Fade"; Nick's
verdict uses **"Forward Lock"** and **"Line Fade."** The founder's latest words
govern; the mockups render his terms. (Flag F1 confirms it in one word.)

**R4 · DRAFT'S ROSTER GROWS; INK STAYS OUT.** Draft adds bulleted lists,
indentation/block quote, line spacing, and text alignment (left · center ·
right, etc.), and carries no Ink. This supersedes Chamber 1's deferral of
alignment/indentation ("until a real need names itself" — the founder just
named it). Lists and block quotes are plain-text honest (`- ` and `> `);
alignment and indentation need a storage convention (flag F3). Line spacing
now has two ruled homes — Draft's tools and the Page menu (R6) — read as two
different things: the ¶ block operation versus the page's leading (flag F4
confirms the split).

**R5 · THE FOOT IS THREE INSTRUMENTS: TYPEWRITER · PROGRESS · FULL SCREEN.**
The separate "Instruments" panel is retired; its options fold into the
others. The desk's fold, decisive and vetoable: **Progress** absorbs the
gear's Progress rows and the whole Instruments panel (Show On/Off · Unit
Lines/Words/Time · Target · Style Bar|Rhizome) plus the session Timer;
**Typewriter** absorbs the typewriter-species rows per R3; **Theme leaves the
foot** for the rail's own theme category (already standing); the goal block
remains the foot's display, its settings living in Progress. **Full Screen**
is ruled verbatim: everything but the page disappears — the browser window
included (OS fullscreen; the cascade's existing FullscreenToggle relocates
here) — exiting by Escape or by an X in the top right that fades in only on
cursor movement; stronger than the ambient fade because chosen; the growing
flow effect still runs unless turned off. This three-instrument foot binds
**every pop-out tool menu** — all modes, all surfaces that carry the foot.

**R6 · THE PAGE MENU IS CHARTERED.** The cascade's Page category gains
general page options governing the open page — margins, line spacing, page
numbers and their placement, headers, footers, etc. — linked to the current
page, resetting to defaults on a new page, with a "set your own defaults"
control at the bottom of the PAGE menu. This is page-level presentation in
the sheet's dimension (Revise keeps the page's *voice* — face and size; the
Page menu owns the page's *sheet* — the split is the reconciliation, flag F5
confirms it). Schema note for the eventual brief: per-page layout fields plus
a user-defaults record are schema-class — the build ticket will carry the
schema flag and Nick's merge word as always; nothing here pre-authorizes it.
The Page face's composition (this new section beside New page · PageFace ·
Places) is Pass 7's territory and renders in the Places pair.

**R7 · MENUS REST OLIVE — the ember leaves the menus.** The menus' accent is
the olive, not brass. This supersedes, *for menus*, the Plateau errata's one
resting-ember allowance: engraved menu headings (STYLING · INK · TYPEWRITER ·
PROGRESS · PAGE · the rest) now rest in olive's engraved register. The
two-regime law otherwise stands — olive marks where you are; and the founding
invariant's evental flash (a sub-second brass on press, "orange marks what you
do") is retained by default and flagged (F7) so one word can strip menus of
brass entirely.

**R8 · INK TYPES ARE ICONS.** Pen, pencil, marker render as icons, not words —
drawn inline (token-colored strokes, no emoji glyphs), each carrying its
accessible name and tooltip so the word survives for the ear and the hover.

**R9 · THE TWO-DRAWER LAW.** The menus are thin enough that on a wide desktop
a left-hand cascade tab (Page · Plan · …) and the Tools sliver stand OPEN AT
ONCE without overlapping — the paper never reflowing, both drawers living in
the measured margin. On a smaller laptop or tablet, where the two would
overlap, opening either CLOSES the other, with the same animated open/close
it performs independently — one motion grammar, whether solo or displacing.
Drawn values in the v2 mockups: cascade panel 200px · sliver 184px (both fit
the wide margin; the 1366 floor runs exclusive). **Constitutional note for
the build brief:** this extends fx18 — the margin now budgets a TWO-drawer
sum at wide, and the three-regime math gains a companion clause (coexistence
threshold = margin ≥ drawer₁ + drawer₂ + gaps). The brief derives it; the
mockups demonstrate it live, including the exclusion handoff's animation.

**R10 · FLUSH DRAWERS, AND THE HANDLE LAW.** Two geometric rulings. (i) An
open drawer sits FLUSH against what it opened from: the cascade's drawer
flush against the rail; the Tools drawer flush against the rail when alone,
flush against the cascade drawer when both stand (the gaps die). (ii) The
Tools grip is a **drawer handle**: affixed to the drawer's left edge, it
travels with the drawer as one object — same movement, **slide only, never a
fade** — and rests flush on the open drawer's left side. The cabinet model
follows: drawers slide OUT from under their left neighbor (rail over cascade
over tools in the stack), the handle protruding at the seam when closed —
which is how a closed drawer stays openable. **Motion grammar split, named:**
the vanish engine keeps its fades for chrome recede/summon; drawer OPEN/CLOSE
is slide-only by this ruling. This supersedes the v2 mockups' fade-assisted
drawer motion and refines the built Sliver's open/close for the eventual
brief (the grip today is stationary while the panel dissolves; ruled: the
handle rides the drawer).

**R11 · THE TOOLS DRAWER IS PAPER-MOUNTED.** Issue 1 was the mockup's own

built Sliver's own anchor, now wearing R10's traveling handle. This

clearing the rail-mounted PAGE drawer's right edge — and the fx18 companion

**THE ANCHOR LAW — ratified from the builder's geometry study.** The v2 pair
as first landed pinned the Tools drawer by *computing* the paper's position
(`Math.min(760, W*0.6)`) while CSS drew the paper at `min(760px, 60ch)` —
two different quantities, so the drawer was pinned to an edge the paper does
not occupy, and the error moved when the webfont failed. The MENU builder's
study (`item83-geometry-study-anchors.html`, its own commit on `main`)
removes the arithmetic instead of correcting it: **anchors are layout —
the Tools dock is a child of the paper's column at `right:100%`, the PAGE
drawer a child of the room at `left:0` — and script measures only to decide
policy (the R9/R11 coexistence handoff). A policy question may measure; an
anchor may not.** Ratified as lane law, fx18's measured-never-assumed
doctrine extended from width to position; the study stands as the lane's
normative reference, its self-checking flush readout adopted into the pair.
**Corollary, from the Board pair's review:** a self-check must compare two
INDEPENDENTLY RENDERED truths. The board's first readout compared a measured
position against the same constant that had assigned it — a check that can
never fail, verifying only that the browser honors `style.left`. The
paper-mounted check could fail (and did) because paper-left and dock-right
render separately. The board pair is rebuilt accordingly: the two drawers
become flex siblings in one `left:0` row, flush falling out of layout with
no constant and no script, and the readout measures `tools-left −
cascade-right` across separately rendered boxes.
The a2/b2 files are re-anchored on this architecture in place — git history
is the annotation; the superseded geometry remains readable at `84302f5`.
