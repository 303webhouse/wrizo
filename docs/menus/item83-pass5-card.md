## FIRST PASS

### THE EXPERTS — what a card's menu is for

**A card is a placement or a note — never a second page.** A16's ontology: derived
membership, authored arrangement. A page-card is a page *seen from* the board; a free
card is the board's own sticky. S13 says the interface let those blur on real
hardware. The menu's deepest obligation on this surface is therefore ontological:
**every control on a card must name its object.** A derived card's controls speak of
the page (open, unpin — membership verbs); a free card's speak of the card (edit,
delete). The "From a page" badge is the noun; the action row is the verbs; S13 is
answered when noun and verbs agree so completely that covering the badge still tells
you what you're holding.

**Proportionate mounting is the species grown up.** BD6 named in-place controls the
object's own hand; the seed's law scales it: the popup's two *edges* become the
card's desk and counsel, under the standing opposition-law — *never two full-height
drawers on a card.* The grammar doesn't shrink onto the card; it re-proportions.

### THE ARCHITECTS — the mapping

**The edges, answered (census §11).** Candidate: the two edge *affordances* — slim
grips, one per side — are always present (they are the popup's frame; G6's constancy
at card scale), and *panels* come on press: partial-height, one section, four rows at
most, riding the one engine. Closed cost at card scale is two quiet grips (G7,
re-proportioned). The right edge sleeps entirely until it has lawful contents — see
counsel below.

**The verbs teach the kind (S13 received).** The action grammar splits by ontology,
and the split is the teaching: on a **derived card**, the desk's rows are membership
verbs — *Open the page* · *Unpin from this board* — and Delete follows BD2's
survivor (categorical-inert with disclosed reason, or absent; his word picks); on a
**free card**, the rows are card verbs — *Edit copy* · *Delete*. The existing badge
and thread footer ride unchanged as the nouns. Nothing here invents a control; it
sorts the censused and the ruled into two honest columns. The *model* questions
underneath — what kinds exist, whether a free card can become a page, where pairing
lives — are item 96's and item 92/93's, behind the seam, through Nick.

**The contested table, settled by kind (CA4).** **Rename:** the slot is mapped (a
first row on the desk edge, whenever it exists) and the *word* stays the Naming
arc's — SV4's seam, ruled with Nick, never built twice. **Color:** two different
proposals have been hiding under one word. Color-as-*decoration* stays trimmed —
Chamber 3's trim stands, and the Plateau ceiling was never going to allow a crayon
box. Color-as-*kind-signal* — tinting by ontology so the board itself teaches S13's
distinction at a glance — is a Places-Model question wearing a menu's clothes, and it
routes to the 96 seam through Nick with that shape named. **Links:** absent, and
rightly — the threads *are* the links; the pin grab makes them and the footer lists
them; a links list in the popup would be the same truth twice (the hands don't
duplicate). **Trash:** governed by BD2's outcome, split by kind as above.

**Card counsel is programmatic-only and sleeps until real (CA5).** The seed's law
stands: cards get programmatic-only counsel in v1 — card-facts of the Map's species
("this card links to no page"), never conversation. Nothing of it is built, so BD4's
law applies at card scale: **no contents, no grip** — the right edge renders nothing
today, and M1 keeps the door unhinted until the capability exists. When the Map
builds, the edge wakes with it.

**The popup's editor is free (CA6).** Bold and Italic wrap selections normally — the
two-press bracket died with Free Write's Format and is not resurrected here — the
eyebrow stays, Close stays brass (CD4.1's word), Esc ownership as censused. The
contenteditable is a card's copy, not a page: no goal block, no instruments, no foot.
A card does not grow the page's furniture; that is half of what S13 means.

### THE OPPOSITION — named chair: Marketing / simplicity

**Objection 1 — two grips on a sticky note is chrome-cancer.** Most cards are three
words and an arrow; framing them in affordances over-dresses the humblest object on
the canvas. **Answer:** the grips are slim, olive-rest, and silent — G7
re-proportioned is precisely the anti-cancer law — and the free card's desk is two
rows. **Conceded honestly:** if the mockups read as dressed-up stickies, the
affordances can go press-revealed (hover/focus) without breaking constancy, and that
variant ships in the Phase 3 pair for exactly this judgment.

**Objection 2 — S13 belongs to item 96, not to a menus pass.** The distinction is a
*model* problem; verbs are cosmetics on confusion. **Answer:** the charter itself
routes S13 to both, seams through Nick — and the halves are separable: 96 decides
what kinds exist and where pages live; this pass makes whatever kinds exist *legible
at the point of touch.* Verbs-teach-kind survives any model 96 lands on.

**Objection 3 — mapping rename's slot while deferring rename is the worst of both.**
The most-wanted control stays unbuilt and now has visible scaffolding in a document.
**Answer:** the slot costs nothing rendered (M1 — nothing shows), and it exists so
the Naming arc's single word drops into a prepared place instead of triggering a
design errand. Deferral with a prepared socket is how this house keeps seams from
becoming forks.

### THE COGNITION / ADHD BENCH

Card scale is the bench's home turf: **one section per edge, four rows maximum, no
scrolling inside a card panel ever** — a limit stops. Verbs name their objects
("Open the page," never "Open") — destination-blind verbs remain the named enemy,
and on this surface they would re-create S13. The kind-split must not read as two
different products: same anatomy, same order, different verbs — difference carried
by words, not by layout. And the popup's arrival/dismissal already owns its motion;
edge panels add none of their own.
