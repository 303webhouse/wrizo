## FIRST PASS

### THE EXPERTS — why the smallest section serves the riskiest mode

**Page-level is the structural anti-loop.** Span-level typography invites an
infinite series of local decisions — the paralysis engine itself. Page-level is
*one decision per page*: the whole page's dress, chosen, done. Chamber 1's
push-back is usually read as a storage argument (it is one), but it is equally
the anti-revision-paralysis thesis wearing law's clothes — the ratified roster
makes the dangerous mode structurally finite.

**"Make this page mine" is identity, not decoration.** The chamber named the
felt want precisely. The Type section is the first door of the user-authored
identity cluster — which is why the custom-font door wakes here and nowhere
else, and why its never-offered discipline matters more than its discoverability.

### THE ARCHITECTS — the mapping

**One Type section, two rows.** Revise's Desk = the **Type** section — **face**
(the page's typeface, drawn from the theme's lawful faces under the cross-theme
prose-pair seam) and **size** — above the shared foot. That is the whole roster.
Alignment and indentation are *absent* — not grayed, not "coming later": the
chamber's own deferral, rendered by G3's law (an unruled capability shows
nothing). The smallest section in the arc, on purpose.

**The sought door renders no row.** Custom font upload lives *inside the type
controls* — Chamber 1's placement — and is **never offered**: no row, no hint,
no locked door wearing paint. "Never offered" is strict; any visible affordance
is an offer. Two lawful seeking shapes are named and neither is chosen: (a) the
face control accepts a typed name, and a name the picker doesn't hold *is* the
seek — the door opens to it ("Use your own — bring a font file"); (b) the writer
asks — the Tutor, asked about fonts, may answer with the door (a said answer is
not an offered row). The gesture's shape is Nick's word with the
identity-backlog seam; the pass maps the door's location and its discipline only.

**The paper is the preview.** Type changes apply to the page live as the writer
adjusts, and revert as cheaply — no modal preview, no Apply ceremony, no
committed-versus-draft state. A decision that is expensive to unmake will not be
made; the bench's law and the anti-paralysis thesis land on the same rule.

**The three truths reconciled (census §9.1 closed).** The seed's matrix included
Revise — that meant *design it*, and this pass is that. The disk defers it — that
stands until the surface builds, the coming-soon tab governed by SP3's outcome
like every dark tab. The agenda walked it — that transfers to the mockup walk:
Revise's Phase 3 pair is the first walkable Revise there has ever been, and the
sitting log's silence on the mode confirms there was nothing else to walk.

**Inheritances ride, restated once:** the shared foot (Time-unit goals serve
revision sessions natively — no change needed); the Typewriter cluster per DR6's
outcome, whichever way Nick's word falls; Publish per DR4's door; nothing
arrives unbidden. The Counsel frame mounts per grammar and its contents stay the

### THE OPPOSITION — named chair: Marketing / simplicity

**Objection 1 — a birth certificate for a room nobody can enter is speculation
compounding.** The surface may build differently than any pass imagines.
**Answer:** the pass binds *only* ratified law into the standing grammar — two
rows and a door. Its cheapness is the design; everything beyond Chamber 1's own
words was refused entry, which is why the objection has nothing larger to point
at.

**Objection 2 — a door nobody can find is a feature nobody uses.** Sought-never-
offered condemns font upload to obscurity. **Answer:** the discipline is M1's
ratified crown jewel, not this pass's invention — and the writer who wants their
own face goes looking, which is the entire theory of user-authored identity.
**Conceded honestly:** the seeking gesture's shape decides whether the door
works, which is exactly why it stays open for Nick rather than guessed here.

**Objection 3 — page-level dress fragments the binder.** Chapter pages in
mismatched faces; the book becomes a ransom note. **Answer, given weight:** a
real risk the chamber's grain ("the whole page's dress") did not rule on.
Whether a binder's pages inherit a shared dress — and whether that is a Places
Model question or a Revise detail — is unruled, and it goes to the lock as
O-RV1 rather than getting invented at the eleventh pass.

### THE COGNITION / ADHD BENCH

Two rows and a door — the bench's ceiling for the paralysis mode is the roster
itself. Live-on-paper preview with instant revert (cheap to unmake is the
condition of getting made). No meta anywhere: no "styles used," no counts, no
per-page dress indicators elsewhere in the chrome. And the coming-soon tab,
until the surface builds, stays visually quiet under SP3's outcome — a dark tab
that draws the eye would tax every session for a room that isn't open.
