## FIRST PASS

### THE EXPERTS — why the canvas changes the questions

**Arrangement is sacred; the hands serve it.** On the page, the Desk acts on text; on
the Board, it acts on membership and arrangement — pinning, connecting, projecting.
The tools that matter are doors-into-furniture ("New page card" creates AND pins in one
act) and arrangement instruments. The grammar's job is unchanged — one form — but the
contents' *kind* shifts from marks to placements.

**The view is not the work.** The Board births a family the page never needed: controls
that change how the work is *seen* without touching what it *is* — item 78's
fit-to-content, and (kin, on script) the binder-view toggle. Per-writer,
presentation-only, always wanted regardless of selection. That triple signature is the
family's definition, and it points at exactly one home in the anatomy.

### THE OPPOSITION — named chair: Marketing / simplicity

**Objection 1 — BD1 pre-empts Nick's hardware.** The ledger said the placement waits
for his hands; a candidate this confident is a decision wearing a question's clothes.
**Answer:** the reservation is restated inside BD1 itself, and the candidate exists so
the sitting tests a *thing* instead of a void — the Phase 3 mockup will put
fit-to-content in the foot at both widths precisely so his hardware can refuse it.

**Objection 2 — the categorical-inert clause is scope creep.** Amending G3 to save one
button is law-making for a corner case. **Answer:** the clause is offered, not
assumed — and the alternative (strict absence) is fully preserved. What the pass
refuses to do is leave a ruled shape and a candidate law silently contradicting each
other on the same surface; that is exactly the rot this lane exists to name.

**Objection 3 — the disk-check stalls the Board.** BD4's "owed before lock" makes the
widest pass wait on a census errand. **Answer:** the check is one read of one panel's
render path, it rides any cycle before Phase 4, and the alternative — locking a frame
law about contents nobody has looked at — is how confident documents get falsified by
screenshots.

### THE COGNITION / ADHD BENCH

The board section is the densest yet (five rows + a mode-gated sixth): still one
section, inside the ceiling, but the bench draws the line here — **no seventh row
without a second section**, and arrangement acts must stay one press (the door rows
already are). The action row's appearance on selection must be motion-quiet (it
arrives because the writer acted; it still may not lunge). Fit-to-content, if BD1
survives the sitting, is one press and *silent* — the view settles without ceremony,
no zoom animation theater. And the counsel grip, while the gate holds and the panel
is contentless, should not invite a press it cannot honor — BD4's presence-follows-
content is a bench law as much as a grammar law.
