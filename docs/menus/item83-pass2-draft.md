### THE EXPERTS — why Draft is the reconciliation room

**Draft tests G2, not G7.** Free Write proved the grammar can be almost nothing; Draft
proves one form can hold real density — two tool sections above a working foot —
without the panel becoming a control panel. The markdown set stays frozen (the storage
law beneath everything: `entry.text` is plain text with conventions; the strips never
demand a storage model the page can't honestly keep).

**Strip questions resolve where the strip is fullest.** Publish-as-tab and the Progress
Bar's placement history are prose-wide questions, but they can only be *argued* against
the densest built state — Draft. Answering them here binds all prose modes (G2), which
is exactly why the answers travel on the lock sheet rather than locking in a pass.

### THE ARCHITECTS — the mapping

**Two sections above the foot.** Draft's Desk panel: the **Format** section and the
**Structure** section, then the shared foot (goal block + instruments row). At the
1366×768 floor this sits inside the bench's three-section ceiling with room to spare.

**Format: the frozen set as rows.** **B** Bold · **I** Italic · **H** Heading · **¶**

**Structure sheds the tablist for a verb.** The built control is a Prose | Screenplay

**"Convert to Screenplay…"** (the script surface mirrors it as "Convert to Prose…").
The surface itself declares where you are — the courier measure announces screenplay
louder than any tab — so the tablist's one honest service (showing current structure)

**The Progress Bar's two rulings, reconciled in sequence (census §9.5).** Chamber 1

### THE OPPOSITION — named chair: Marketing / simplicity

**Objection 1 — this pass rubber-stamps the built.** Draft maps nearly as-is; where's
the argument? **Answer:** in the three reconciliations. Draft is where Publish's dress,
Structure's dress, and the Progress Bar's two rulings had to be argued, because Draft
is the fullest room they all stand in. The mapping being clean elsewhere is the grammar
working, not the committee sleeping.

**Objection 2 — re-dressing working controls spends change on nothing.** Publish and
Structure both function today; door-dress and verb-dress are aesthetics. **Answer:**
they are law-compliance, not aesthetics — mode clothes on non-modes is the exact
confusion BM1 exists to prevent, and the Board already paid for this precedent. The
cost is one Phase-3 mockup each; both dresses will be shown, not asserted.

**Objection 3 — strikethrough's "ready row" is pressure.** Mapping the row before
Nick's word makes yes the path of least resistance. **Answer, conceded in part:** the
mapping exists so his *no* is equally cheap — the lock sheet carries yes/no as equal
single words, and the pass states plainly that silence means the row does not mount.

### THE COGNITION / ADHD BENCH

Two sections + foot sits inside the density ceiling at the floor width — no scroll, no
collapse forced. The Format glyphs must be instant-read (B · I · H · ¶, one row, states
visible). The conversion verb must name its destination in the control itself

bench's named enemy. Dimmed-syntax register unchanged is re-affirmed as a bench law,
not just a storage law: the conventions reading as craft is what keeps typed markdown
from feeling like code.

**The reasoning, recorded because it is the load-bearing part.** The

suppressing counsel everywhere; it was about not letting counsel reach the
writer while they are generating. Drawing the boundary at the mode does that
exactly, and buys Revise its own voice.
