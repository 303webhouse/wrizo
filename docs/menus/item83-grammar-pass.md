## FIRST PASS

### THE EXPERTS — why a menu is what it is here

**The third kind.** BM1 is law: doors are doors, modes are modes — and a tool control is
neither. The census catches the current build squatting controls in both: the mode strip
hosts Publish (an action wearing a tab), the foot hosts instruments, "Forward lock" sits
where a ruled cluster should be. Name the third kind: the **instrument**. An instrument
changes neither where you are (a door's work) nor what the surface is (a mode's work) —
it acts on the work, or reports it. Instruments live in **hands**. The whole arc is the
act of giving the third kind its own constitutional home so the first two stop absorbing
refugees.

**Two hands, asymmetric by constitution.** The Desk side acts on the page; the Counsel
side speaks about it and is barred from ever writing into it (A12–A15, architectural in
the code — the Tutor holds no editor reference at all). This asymmetry is not styling.
The grammar's deepest obligation is that a press on the left can **change** the page and
a press on the right can only **say** — while the two hands still read as one body.
Geometry mirrors; powers don't.

**Closed cost is the budget.** Wrizo funnels a writer forward; menus exist for the
back-out moments and must cost nothing during the funnel. The vanish engine already
prices chrome this way — presence is earned by being wanted. The grammar's first number
is therefore what a **closed** menu system costs the page: it should cost exactly its
grips, and nothing else.

**One engine, one arrow grammar.** SV5 verbatim: every surface gets left-hand tools and
right-hand counsel, one drawer engine, one arrow grammar, no floating arrows. The census
holds six inventories — those are six **contents**. This pass is the one **form**.

### THE ARCHITECTS — the anatomy, named

**THE GRIP.** The persistent affordance; one per hand per surface. The Sliver's grip is
the type specimen: minimal, edge-mounted, persists when its panel dissolves, keyboard
chord `Ctrl/Cmd + /`. A grip never relocates or morphs within a surface — the hand must
know where the hand is. The right hand's chord is **not invented here**: the census shows
the Tutor's two anchors but no ruled chord; that gap is flagged OPEN below, not filled.

**THE PANEL.** Rides the one vanish engine — no second dissolve dialect. Width obeys the
three-regime law (constitutional, fx18): measured true geometric margin, the regime-2
downward discontinuity, and below the usable-panel floor an open panel overlays at natural
width as documented degradation. `DOCK_FLOOR_PX` stands. **Paper never reflows for
chrome** — a panel occupies measured margin or overlays; it never pushes the page.

**THE SECTION.** A panel's contents group under engraved zone headings — and the
engraving is the **one** resting-ember allowance the Plateau errata grants. Sections
collapse; expansion is one press; collapsed state is remembered per surface. A section is
where a mode's contents live; what persists across modes lives in the foot.

**THE ROW.** One control: label plus affordance, resolving in **one press**. The
two-press bracket (Bold/Italic wrapping a selection) is the ruled exception that proves
the rule — one gesture in two presses, already law. No press-to-arm-then-press patterns
anywhere else.

**THE FOOT.** The panel's ground floor — the instrument strip the census already found
(goal block, gear, typewriter toggle). Per-surface instruments persist in the foot;
per-mode contents live in the sections above it. The foot is why switching modes doesn't
cost the writer their bearings.

**DISCLOSURE DEPTH.** Panel, plus at most **one** pop-out beyond it — the gear opening
SettingsPanel is the specimen and the ceiling. Anything deeper becomes in-panel
disclosure. Nested cascades never.

**ARROWS.** On grips and panel edges only. No floating arrows — SV5's own words.

### THE OPPOSITION — named chair: Marketing / simplicity

**Objection 1 — grammar-first risks uniform-but-dead.** Writers press things; they don't
read constitutions. If the Free Write strip and the Card popup wear identical anatomy,
the Card — whose desk is two edges of a popup — gets heavier than proportionate mounting
allows. **Concession of record, ranked in:** contents-over-form is the ranking. No
control moves homes to satisfy symmetry, and the Card inherits the grammar **scaled** —
its edges are its desk; a full panel anatomy is not owed there and the Card pass argues
the scaling, not this pass.

**Objection 2 — the mirror is dogma.** Counsel is conversational; forcing section/row
anatomy onto a chat surface is furniture for furniture's sake. **Partially conceded:**
the Tutor stays conversation-first — the census shows "Talk it through" leading and that
survives. The grammar binds the Counsel side's **frame** (grip, panel regimes, sections
for lenses and the Bible, the foot's cost meter) — never its dialogue.

**Objection 3 — freeze theater.** Nothing builds until post-vacation; specifying form now
front-loads what the sitting should inform. **Rejected:** the sitting gate exists so this
document stays CANDIDATE. Arguing form once, now, is precisely what makes six context
passes cheap and the post-vacation briefs decision-complete.

### THE COGNITION / ADHD BENCH

**Density ceiling.** At the 1366×768 floor, an open panel shows at most **three expanded
sections** without scrolling. Past that, collapse beats scroll: scanning engraved
headings costs less than scrolling open contents. And a limit **stops** — overflow never
relocates a control to some other home.

**Motion.** Panel arrival uses the existing vanish timing; no novel animation dialects
enter through this arc. The grip never moves.

**Memory.** Expansion state persists per surface across sessions. Re-scanning is the
attention tax this bench exists to name; memory pays it once. This also keeps the
progressive-disclosure law honest — what a writer opened stays open for them, and what
they never sought never appears (M1: no locked doors, and no doors they didn't ask for).

## SECOND PASS — CANDIDATES

Argued above; stated here as the rulings Nick will lock, amend, or strike. Basis in
parentheses.

**G1 · THE THIRD KIND.** A tool control is an *instrument* — neither door nor mode; its
home is a hand, assigned by function, argued never assumed. (BM1 extended.)

**G2 · ONE FORM, SIX CONTENTS.** The grammar is invariant across all six contexts;
contexts vary contents only. Any divergence travels to Nick by name. (SV5.)

**G3 · ABSENT, NOT DISABLED.** Unbuilt or ratified-unbuilt capability is **absent** from
menus. *Disabled* is reserved for transient gates on real capability — the
selection-gated action row and the system-board sliver section are the two specimens the
census found, and they are the whole pattern. A grayed-out control for an unbuilt feature
is a locked door wearing paint. (M1.)

**G4 · ONE POP-OUT DEEP.** Panel plus at most one pop-out; deeper structure becomes
in-panel disclosure. (Bench + SettingsPanel specimen.)

**G5 · THE EMBER CEILING.** Engraved section headings are the sole resting-ember site;
every interactive state rides olive-rest / brass-hover / orange-press. Zero resting
orange anywhere in a menu. (Plateau errata; the lane law.)

**G6 · GRIP CONSTANCY.** One grip per hand per surface; a grip never relocates or morphs
within its surface. (Sliver specimen; bench.)

**G7 · CLOSED COST = ONE GRIP.** A closed menu system costs the page exactly its grips.
Everything else vanishes by the one engine. (Vanish-engine kinship; SV5's one-drawer.)

**G8 · POWERS DON'T MIRROR.** Geometry mirrors across the hands (fx18); capability never
does — left presses act on the page, right presses speak about it. (A12–A15 restated as
grammar.)
