His six sections, in his order. Current Page — the tiny label is the superscript; the name is the element, edited here or in the title bar by one control, with 179's star beside it. Add to Board and Page Settings drill in (item 166: a menu about the page never covers the page — the readout shows the gap). Open Pages is FIX's built section: three rows visible, the rest scrolled, ordered by edit, starred first. Flip (ii) to "cap of three" and the fourth page becomes unreachable — the reason this desk agrees with FIX's viewport. Flip (iii) and the typeface control leaves Page Settings while Typewriter is ON, because a typewriter does not offer you a typeface; size and spacing stay. Flip (i) to see the two readings of "Untitled": a placeholder for an unwritten page (item 136 stands), or a stored default name (136 reversed). The last button reads "New Page" — singular: 178 renames the noun, not the count.
