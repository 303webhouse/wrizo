Book
The same board, under two types. As a Book it shows one page at a time and you flip (← →, or the arrows); ＋ page makes a new surface in the spine, not a card on a table. ＋ card puts a card on the page you are on — and it stays there: add one, flip away, flip back. Switch the rig to Default and the same contents lie on a table top, every page a card, arranged where they were put. Bottom right: the amended thumbnail law — the same board is drawn wide as a Default and tall as a Book, because a board wears the proportion of its type. 172-Q6 is live in the rig: a card on the page (where you put it, as ink sits) or in the margin (it can never cover a word). The writing is still on the page; the Book is the container that shows it — the canon holds.
