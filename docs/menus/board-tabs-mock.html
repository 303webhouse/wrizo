Press any tab — the board changes over and the olive marker moves. In the stable order the tabs stay exactly where they were (the rig counts how many moved); switch the rig to relative and press again to feel the rival: the row regroups around wherever you stand. A board inside another sits right after it, joined beneath it by a thin line; a board from another drawer carries “in Research” — the same preposition as item 163’s fix. Press ＋ to put a board inside the one you are on: the list opens inside the row, never over the board. Winterfell is already inside Stark and says so; Westeros contains Stark, so it cannot go inside it — present, inert, and it says why (item 128’s cycle guard, met where you meet it). Pick Lore: its card appears here, and its tab joins beneath Stark’s.
