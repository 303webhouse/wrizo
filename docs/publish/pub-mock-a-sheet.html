Publish → opens a sheet beside the page. The page never moves or closes: the green readout above measures it with the sheet open and shut. The sheet asks three things. What: from a selection or one card up to the whole work, or Everything. As: editions that change with the kind of writing (try the Kind buttons). To: Download, Share, Copy, WordPress (as a draft), Substack (by paste) or X (one post at a time). One orange button does it; Again repeats your last choice. Each edition says what it leaves out. At 1100 wide the margin is narrower than the sheet, so it lies over the page's edge instead of pushing it. Try a board card to publish a single card. Committee lean: build this first; the Press (mockup B) comes later for whole books.
