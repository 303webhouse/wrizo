Nick's pick: B only. Publish → opens the Press, a place you pass through, never a home. It opens at the size of what you're on: switch you came from to a board card and it opens on just that card, ready to post to X or save as a quote card in two taps. The chip at top left takes you straight back to where you were. Left: the running order: title page, dedication, chapters, sources. Tick what goes in; open a chapter to see its sections; click a row to jump the preview there, or Just this to publish that one piece. The order itself comes from your binder, so change it in Plan. Centre: the finished thing at true page shape; flip with ‹ ›. Right: the edition (it changes with the kind of writing), up to three dress choices, and where it goes. One orange button makes it. The unticked line under it moves the book to your Library afterwards, only if you tick it. Try each Kind, and the manuscript's DOCX / PDF, the screenplay's Fountain, and the thesis's Style.
