## 0. In one breath
Read's Home is **one page in every theme**: the writer's **floor** (the commons, where everyone's published work lies), her **guilds** beside it, the **reading desk** when she opens a piece, and the **notes** that come back to her. Each theme dresses that page with its own tokens and calls the four things by its own words; the structure, the geometry of the reading surface and the laws never change. Brass marks only her own acts (publish, reply, join); each theme's guidance colour marks the doors.

## 1. The one structure (what every theme shows, in this order)

| **The writer's guilds** | the groups she belongs to (SOCIAL's word may differ): each a row with its name, its floor (public / gated) and what's new there, **as a quiet mark, never a count** | one list; gated floors show a plain lock word, not a glyph alone |
| **The reading desk** | the piece opened: Write's read-only page package at the law's page rect, Read's highlights, notes and pens on top | the page rect and the page's ink are Write's; the writer's own theme dresses the room around it |
| **The notes** | what came back to her: replies, highlights, workshop notes, as a list | in Read: a list; in Write: only inside the drawer (ruled Oct 31), never a popup, never a count |
| **The doors** | Publish to Read (from Write), Join a guild, Reply, Highlight | brass = her act; a door = the theme's guidance colour; destructive words plain (Leave, Remove, Report) |

**Laws carried over:** page text ≥ 13.9:1 on the reading desk, labels ≥ 4.5:1; reduced motion = a still state; ≤ 3 flashes/s; no engagement bait; the Room setting applies (Read applies the same clock rule locally, identity rev d); a theme's Read surfaces follow its redesign (live themes dress Read with today's tokens until then).
