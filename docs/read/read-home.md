**Wrizors lane · Nov 2, 2026 · draft 3 folds SOCIAL's three asks (relayed by Fable, Nov 2 4:45 pm): no reading length on any Read surface; the Reading Room as its own row apart from the commons; words per theme for Read's extra regions.** What this doc is: the one structure of Read's Home, and the five skins over it. What it is not: Read's information architecture (SOCIAL owns that: feeds, guilds, gates, workshops, classes); where SOCIAL's IA names a region differently, SOCIAL's name wins and the skin rows apply to it. Where this doc describes what a region *is*, it is the Wrizors' reading of SOCIAL's names, for SOCIAL to correct. **Nothing here is new data in Write;** Read reads the writer's theme id, `theme.room` and `theme.switches` from the identity claims (identity draft 0 rev d) and dresses itself. This copy is self-contained: every theme's Read section is inline in §5.

## 0. In one breath
Read's Home is **one page in every theme**: **the Reading Room** (published, vouched work), **the commons** (drafts open to every writer, read blind), the writer's **guilds** beside them, the **reading desk** when she opens a piece, and the **notes** that come back to her. Each theme dresses that page with its own tokens and calls the things on it by its own words; the structure, the geometry of the reading surface and the laws never change. Brass marks only her own acts (publish, reply, join, vouch); each theme's guidance colour marks the doors. **No number sits beside any piece, anywhere in Read** (no reading length, no scores, likes, counts).

## 2b. Read's extra regions: the words per theme (SOCIAL's ask 3)
**Three rules decide which words change and which stay plain.**
1. **Arbor speaks the house words.** Arbor is the default theme and SOCIAL's house words (table, room, roundtable, the commons) already sound like Arbor's study; renaming them would only add words to learn.
2. **Plain in every theme: any word that grants or asks consent, carries safety, or faces the public.** That is the three presence states (someone "free to talk" is consenting to be approached, and minors may be present in classes), **Messages**, **a class** and **Enroll in a class** (teachers and students must find them under one name), **the vouch** (a trust act; a misread costs trust), and **the Reading Room's public page** (a visitor from outside has no theme, so it wears Read's own house look and words). Destructive words stay plain, as before.
3. **A theme renames a region only where its lexicon already has the word** (Machina's Files and Groups; Flux's Nets, Drops and Signals), so the theme reads as one language in Write and Read alike.
