WRIZO // FLUX MARK

Three Wrizo logos in Flux's style. In each, the last letter is not drawn: the O is the writer's cursor — orange, the one colour Flux keeps for you, blinking at the Flux caret's own rate (1.05 s, well under 3 Hz; still under reduced motion). Each is shown large on the Flux ground, on the light page, as an app icon and at 16 px.

Why it works: it is the WRIZO wordmark Flux already shows (Rajdhani, tracked, top-left of every screen), with its O replaced by the block caret the Flux page already draws. Nothing new to learn: the logo is the moment before you type. The faint cyan offset on the letters is the canon's chromatic hairline, never red.

Why it works: it is drawn on the same pixel grid as Flux's own icons (the docs, circuits and log glyphs in the rail), so the mark and the interface are one family. The cursor is a block of pixels in the O's place; at 16 px it survives as "W" plus an orange block. Pixels never smooth (crisp edges at every size).

Arbor keeps the hand-drawn original; Flux shows the same hand, jacked in.
