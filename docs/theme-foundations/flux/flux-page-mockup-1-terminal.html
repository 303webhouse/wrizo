Page mockup 1 — TERMINAL

The Flux page as a terminal, in the language of the Schema circuit. The page is dark glass with bracket corners, the only stable thing on the screen: the ground's circuitry and glitches never enter it (canon §6), and they damp to almost nothing the moment you type.

  • The left rail is the board's command boxes ("Locate last node", "Fit circuit"), stacked as the rail: text boxes with a key, no icons. The place you are in is the orange box at the top; the current list is cyan. "Return to last node" sits in the rail, in orange, from every surface.
  • The sprint bar glitches its letters: the readout ("OVERCLOCK // SPRINT 214 / 300") scrambles toward code and resolves back, the same autocorrect-and-rejection the circuit nodes do. At the goal: the ignition sweep, the bar hands off to orange, and the letters scramble once and resolve to CHECKPOINT. Never above 3 Hz; still when reduced motion is on.
  • The two hands (TOOLS left, OPERATOR right) and the + square right of the page match the circuit: + connects this doc to a new or existing circuit or doc, opens one, or disconnects it. In the build, what + opens comes up in a split pane (see Schema v2).
  • While you write, every piece of chrome recedes to 6%; the page stays.

For Fable

The page's size and position are untouched by every control here (page primacy). The rail as text boxes changes the rail's look, not its places. The hands are the existing drawers, redrawn.
