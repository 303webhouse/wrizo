## §2 · THE VANISH, PROVEN — NOT A RACE AT ALL

The falsification the ruling asked for. Raw-seed a row, make **one** ordinary
product write, read storage back:

| step | rows in storage |
|---|---|
| after the raw write | `["raw-row"]` |
| after one `wrizoCreateJournalPage` call | `["seam-row"]` — **`raw-row` GONE** |
| after reload | `["seam-row"]` |

**Every product write serialises the WHOLE in-memory cache back over storage,
and the cache never contained the raw row.** The rows were never late. They were
**overwritten**.

That single fact explains everything the ticket found puzzling:

- **why the detail was always exactly `before=false after=false paired=false`** —
  only the `after` term can be wrong, because the row is simply absent;
- **why the 4000ms settle poll was insufficient by construction** — there was
  nothing to wait for, and no amount of polling reaches a row that no longer
  exists;
- **why it looked ~50%** — the vanish needs a product write to land *after* the
  seed in the same run, so it fires on run shape, not on timing;
- **why isolation passed 3/3** — a fresh desk makes no intervening product write.

**Measured before/after: ~50% (2 fails in 4 here, 5 in 10 at ERRATA) → 6 runs,
6 passes, 36/36.**

## §3 · ONE CORRECTION TO THE RULING'S PREMISE

**"Migration, not invention" held only half**, and the half that failed is the
whole reason this bug survived as long as it did.

The seam existed — but **it could not express the fixture**. `JournalPageSeed`
carried `{id, text, createdAt, strokes}`, while `bm1` needs `origin:'loose'`,
`pageType:'board'`, `projectId` and `boxes`.

**`origin` is the load-bearing one.** It is written **only at birth** — nothing
in the app ever changes it afterwards — and `belongsOnShelf` excludes anything
journal-homed (`persistence.ts:1314`). So a seam that could not seed
`origin:'loose'` **could not produce a Shelf-eligible entry at all**, which is
precisely the property S2 asserts. `bm1` was not reaching past the seam out of
habit. It was reaching past it because the seam could not do the job.

**The lesson worth keeping, because it generalises past this ticket: a seam that
cannot say what a fixture needs is not bypassed loudly. It is bypassed QUIETLY**
— with a raw write that works perfectly until some unrelated write flushes over
it, and then presents as a flaky test that no polling can fix.

The repair widens `JournalPageSeed` with **origin / pageType / projectId /
boxes**, each applied **only when supplied** — the discipline `strokes` already
established, so an unseeded call writes the byte-identical row it always did.
**Product code passes no seed at all**, so no shipped behaviour is reachable
from this change.

## §4 · THE REPAIR, IN ORDER

1. **Widen the seed** (above).
2. **Migrate `bm1`'s `seedEntries`** to `window.wrizoCreateJournalPage`.
3. **Absorb the one behavioural difference the seam's own comment names:** the
   seam write is **debounced** (300ms) where the raw write was synchronous, so
   the fixture now waits for the flush to land **before** reloading. A reload
   that outran it would hydrate a store missing the rows just seeded — trading
   one vanish for another.
4. **LIFT THE PARK.** The original assertion is restored **at its own place in
   the run**, not relocated somewhere quieter. Act 1's deterministic stand-in is
   removed; `bm1` parks nothing again.

## §5 · WHAT ACT 1 FOUND THAT THE TICKET DID NOT PREDICT

Recorded because it changed the ticket, and because it is the reason act 2 aimed
at seeding rather than at S2.

Act 1 parked the orphan check and then **verified the park** — four runs: three
green, one **red at 2/35, on two DIFFERENT S2 checks**, neither the orphan
(`"S2 explicit pairing"`, detail empty; `"S2 unpair"`, **detail `null`**).
`rawEntryStr` returns `null` only when the row is **absent from localStorage**.

So the non-determinism was never "the orphan check flakes" — **bm1's seeded rows
were vanishing wholesale**, and the orphan check was merely where it showed
most often. **The park alone would not have stopped the coin flip**, and act 1's
own record says so rather than claiming the ticket closed.

**Named honestly: two of the 54 are this lane's own** — `underline.mjs` and
