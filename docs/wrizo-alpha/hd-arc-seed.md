## The charge

Nick's verdict SV5, ratified: *"Every writing surface needs a lefthand
popout menu and a righthand popout menu. Every LHP is a set of tools
and settings specific to the surface it's attached to. Every RHP
offers relevant and harnessed AI or programmatic ('smart') options.
This includes Cards, which currently do not have anything like this.
And we don't have floating arrows all over the place like we do
currently."*

Chamber B universalized this into grammar: **the left hand is the
desk** (tools the writer wields); **the right hand is the counsel**
(things that speak when spoken to). The Page already lives this way —
tool strip left, Tutor right. Your arc makes it law for Page, Script,
Board, Card, and Ink/Image, on **one drawer engine, one arrow
grammar**. House names for Nick's ear: **the Desk side** and **the
Counsel side**; LHP/RHP remain the spec terms.

## The hard rails — inherited whole, not renegotiated per surface

- **A12–A15 are constitutional.** Every Counsel side is born inside the
  Tutor's rails: never writes, never inserts, nothing sent unless
  asked. Extending counsel to new surfaces multiplies the ghostwriter's
  attack surface; the constitution does not get re-argued at each one.
- **Cards get programmatic-only counsel in v1** — no per-card AI. The
  smaller attack surface is the point.
- **Board counsel is deferred** to a ratified disclosure-v4 sentence;
  **no board text rides to a model until Nick ratifies that sentence.**
  Programmatic board counsel (the Map, unlinked cards, connections) is
  in scope now.
- **Wrizo never generates images.** The app never holds the pen in any
  medium.
- **Anti-gamification:** nothing counted, no completion states, no
  badges, no meters in any panel.
- **The lane law:** olive at rest, brass on hover, orange only for
  humans acting. No at-rest affordance in the brass lane.
- **Proportionate mounting:** on small surfaces the two hands are the
  two EDGES of the existing popup — a card's popup becomes its desk.
  Two full-height drawers on a card is chrome inflation and Marketing's
  objection was sustained.

## The thesis, for when a design decision is genuinely close

The app funnels the writer forward into their words and lets them back
out to reorganize whenever they want. The Desk side is where the
writer's own hands do work. The Counsel side speaks only when spoken
to and never holds the pen. If a panel makes the page harder to reach,
it is wrong however useful it seems.
