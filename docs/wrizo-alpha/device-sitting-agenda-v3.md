## PART 1 — THE DAILY LOOP (walk it three times)
Cold open — where does it put you? New page, five minutes of REAL
prose. Typewriter feel, paper stays put, nothing steals your eye.
Leave, return — text there, caret sane. Find the page again through
all three doors: Drawer, Journal, cascade. Repeat in Draft, then
Revise — each mode gives what it promised, withholds what it
promised to withhold.

## PART 2 — THE P2 HOUSE, ticket by ticket

FX16 — THE SILENT INVITE. A fresh empty page shows no "Write…"
    placeholder, no first-line whisper. Truly quiet.
FX18 — THE THREE REGIMES. Open the Tutor panel on a page at your
    normal width: it occupies the MARGIN, flush against the paper,
    never covering it, grip always reachable. Narrow the window
    hard: below the floor it overlays instead — deliberate, not a
    bug. Open it on a BOARD: it overlays at natural width, grip
    riding on top. Arrow mirrors the sliver. Board top menu:
    ALL-CAPS, right-aligned. Check at your laptop size AND wide.
BG2 — BEGINNINGS YOU CAN READ. Fresh board, fresh page: the
    beginnings row. Your eye rules — legible in your actual light,
    or still muddy?
PB1 — BORN ON THE FIRST WORD. (a) Open a New Page from every door
    you use — Catch, Write, cascade, a drawer — and WALK AWAY
    without typing. Places, Journal, Shelf stay clean. No Untitled
    litter, ever again. (b) Come back, type fast from the very
    first keystroke — a full sentence, no pause. Every character
    lands. Watch the address flip from door to room with no
    flicker, no focus loss. (c) Star and tags are ABSENT on an
    unwritten page and return the moment it has a word. (d) Reload
    an unwritten page before typing — the door still means what it
    meant. (e) OBS-1, ten seconds: new page → choose Screenplay →
    does the room turn Courier IMMEDIATELY, no reload? (f) Fresh
    board: nothing exists until the first box. PLAN→ on a fresh
    page: exactly one board, paired.
FX17 — THE BOARD'S FLOOR (P2b only). Drag a card downward: the
    canvas grows with it, smoothly — the old stutter is dead. It
    grows only, never shrinks mid-drag. At three page-widths tall:
    a hard, exact stop — no dead zone, no spring-back. Grab THREE
    cards and drag down: they stop as a group, shape intact. A card
    from an old board sitting deeper than the limit: touch it, drag
    it sideways — it must NOT be yanked upward. The limit stops; it
    never relocates.
