## The page's rectangle

At 1366 × 768 the page is 620 × 640 at left 415, top 64. One CSS rule (`.page` in `reorg.css`) sets it. The only thing a script sets is the page's left: 415 at 1366 wide, and, in a split only, the middle of the page's own side (left 100 at half). The page's width, height and top are the same in a split as out of it. Everything that opens (pop-outs, the two side menus, the list of Parts, the search) is laid over the room or beside the page, never in the page's flow. A Board on its own is laid over the page (left 116, top 64, 1218 × 640 at 1366 × 768, as frame P3-NB-C draws it): the page is still there underneath, at 415 / 64 / 620 × 640, with its Part, its scroll and its words as she left them. The search marks found words inside the page; a mark changes no letter's width, so the words do not re-wrap. A stage changes nothing of the page's rectangle: in SPRINT the chrome around it fades (opacity only), and in FINALIZE the whole Doc scrolls inside it, under a label in its own top margin. "What changed with this stage" is laid over the page's middle, under the top bar, without touching it.
