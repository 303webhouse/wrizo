# Team A, "The quiet desk": every new or moved word

Every line here is **PROPOSED**. Nothing is decided until the Wrizors pass it through the lexicon.

No ruled word was renamed. The rail (DOCS, PROJECTS, UNFILED, THEME, CONTROLS), the tabs (TEXT · INK · PARTS, PLAN · ASSETS · FEEDBACK), the stages (SPRINT · BUILD · EDIT · FINALIZE) and every offer of the stage table read exactly as in the prototype, in the same capitals.

| Word, as drawn | Where | Note |
|---|---|---|

| **More for** *(an asset's name)* | The spoken label of a Board row's own menu (three dots) | PROPOSED. Heard by a screen reader; the pointer sees "More". |

| **NR**, **P**, **D** | The letters on a review flag | PROPOSED. NR is NOT RULED, P is PROPOSED, D is [DATA]. Not the product. |
| **review note** | The head of an open flag: "NOT RULED · review note" | PROPOSED. Not the product. |
| **page 2 of 3** | The readout under the page, in place of "1.6 pages" | PROPOSED. The brief's own example. |
